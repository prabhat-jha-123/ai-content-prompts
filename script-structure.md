# Script Structure

Frameworks and structures designed for high-retention short-form and cinematic YouTube content.

---

# Short-Form Structure

## 1. Hook (0-3 Seconds)

Goal:
Immediately capture attention.

Examples:
- strongest visual first
- loudest sound first
- fastest movement first
- curiosity statement

---

## 2. Build-Up (3-10 Seconds)

Goal:
Increase tension and anticipation.

Techniques:
- fast cuts
- cinematic transitions
- synchronized music
- visual pacing

---

## 3. Peak Moment (10-20 Seconds)

Goal:
Deliver the strongest visual/audio payoff.

Examples:
- exhaust sound
- cinematic reveal
- transformation sequence
- dramatic scene

---

## 4. Ending (Final Seconds)

Goal:
Leave strong final impression.

Techniques:
- final cinematic shot
- beat sync
- smooth outro
- replay-worthy ending

---

# Retention Strategy

- Keep cuts fast and intentional
- Avoid dead moments
- Match visuals with audio beats
- Use contrast and motion
- Maintain visual intensity
- Start with strongest scene immediately

---

# Thumbnail Strategy

- High contrast visuals
- Strong subject focus
- Minimal distractions
- Cinematic lighting
- Aggressive composition
- Emotion-driven imagery

---

# Audience Psychology

Focus Areas:
- curiosity
- power
- luxury
- speed
- emotion
- cinematic immersion
- visual satisfaction
